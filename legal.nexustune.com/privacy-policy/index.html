Privacy Policy

Nexus Tune Official Legal & Compliance Document • Published 2026

Welcome to Nexus Tune.

This Privacy Policy ("Privacy Policy") explains how Nexus Tune ("Nexus Tune", "Company", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, processes, shares, protects, and otherwise handles your personal information when you access or use our website, dashboard, mobile applications (if any), digital platforms, APIs, software, music distribution services, publishing administration services, rights management services, royalty management services, or any other products and services offered by Nexus Tune (collectively, the "Services").

Nexus Tune is committed to protecting your privacy and processing personal information responsibly and transparently in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

By accessing our website, creating an account, purchasing a subscription, uploading Content, or using any of our Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, you must discontinue use of our Services.

1. Company Information

For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, Nexus Tune operates from its Principal Head Office located at:

Nexus Tune, Carrer del Bruc, Santa Coloma de Gramenet, Barcelona – 08923, Spain

Nexus Tune may maintain regional offices, representatives, contractors, or operational teams in other countries for customer support, administration, marketing, technical assistance, and related operational purposes.

However, the Principal Head Office in Spain shall remain the primary place of business for administration of this Privacy Policy unless otherwise required by applicable law.

2. Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all individuals who interact with Nexus Tune, including:

Website visitors; Artists, Labels, Rights holders, Songwriters, Publishers, Producers, Composers, Subscribers, Business customers, Partners; and Any other users of our Services.

This Privacy Policy applies whether you access Nexus Tune through: Our website, Dashboard, Mobile applications (if available), APIs, Customer support, Email communications; or Other official Nexus Tune platforms.

3. Definitions

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy:

"Account" means your registered Nexus Tune user account.

"Content" means any audio recording, music video, artwork, metadata, lyrics, composition, publishing information, contributor information, AI-generated material, promotional material, or other content submitted to Nexus Tune.

"Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual, as defined under applicable data protection laws.

"Processing" includes collecting, recording, organizing, storing, using, transmitting, sharing, deleting, or otherwise handling Personal Data.

"DSP" means Digital Service Providers including Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Amazon Music, TikTok, Meta, Deezer, Pandora, VEVO, Boomplay, Anghami, JioSaavn, Gaana, Wynk, and other supported digital platforms.

4. Information We Collect

Nexus Tune may collect Personal Data directly from you, automatically through your use of the Services, or from third-party sources where permitted by law.

The categories of information we collect may include: Identity Information, Contact Information, Account Information, Payment Information, Tax Information, Music Metadata, Rights Ownership Information, Publishing Information, Device Information, Usage Information, Technical Information, Communications, Customer Support Records, Marketing Preferences; and Any other information you voluntarily provide.

5. Information You Provide

Depending on the Services you use, you may provide information including: Identity Information, Full Name, Stage Name, Company Name, Label Name, Date of Birth (where required), Government-issued identification (where required for verification), Contact Information, Email Address, Telephone Number, Postal Address, Country, State, City, Account Information, Username, Password, Profile Details, Language Preferences, Notification Preferences, Financial Information

Where applicable, payment-related information may include: Bank Account Details, PayPal Account, Payment Recipient Information, Tax Identification Numbers, VAT or GST Information, Other payment information necessary for royalty payments or subscription billing.

Payment card information may be processed by authorized third-party payment providers and may not be stored directly by Nexus Tune.

6. Music & Rights Information

To provide our Services, Nexus Tune may collect information relating to your Content, including: Track Titles, Album Titles, Artist Names, Contributor Credits, Composer Information, Songwriter Information, Producer Information, Publishing Information, Ownership Splits, Copyright Information, ISRCs, UPCs, Lyrics, Cover Artwork, Audio Files, Video Files, Release Dates, Genres, Languages, AI Content Disclosures, Licensing Information; and Other metadata reasonably necessary to distribute, administer, protect, or monetize your Content.

7. Information Collected Automatically

When you access our Services, Nexus Tune may automatically collect certain technical information, including: Internet Protocol (IP) Address, Browser Type, Operating System, Device Identifiers, Device Type, Screen Resolution, Language Settings, Time Zone, Referral URLs, Pages Visited, Time Spent on Pages, Clickstream Information, Login Activity, Error Logs, Crash Reports, Security Logs, Session Information; and Other technical diagnostic information.

Such information helps us improve the security, functionality, and performance of our Services.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Nexus Tune uses cookies, pixels, web beacons, SDKs, local storage, and similar technologies to:

Authenticate users: Maintain login sessions, Improve website functionality, Analyze website traffic, Measure performance, Detect fraud, Enhance security, Remember user preferences; and Deliver relevant communications where permitted by applicable law.

Additional information regarding cookies is available in our separate Cookie Policy.

9. Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from third-party sources including:

Payment processors; Digital Service Providers (DSPs), Music industry partners, Collection societies, Publishing administrators, Rights management partners, Identity verification providers, Fraud prevention providers, Analytics providers, Marketing partners; and Publicly available sources.

We process such information only where permitted by applicable law and for purposes compatible with this Privacy Policy.

10. Legal Bases for Processing (Where Applicable)

Where required under applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Nexus Tune processes Personal Data on one or more of the following legal bases:

Performance of a contract; Compliance with legal obligations, Legitimate business interests, Your consent, where required, Protection of vital interests; o, Other lawful bases recognized under applicable law.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time where permitted by law.

11. How We Use Your Information

Nexus Tune processes Personal Data only for legitimate business purposes and in accordance with applicable law.

We may use your Personal Data to: Create and manage your Account, Provide subscription-based Services, Process music distribution requests, Deliver Content to Digital Service Providers (DSPs), Administer publishing services where applicable, Process royalty reporting and payments, Verify your identity, Prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activities, Respond to customer support requests, Improve the functionality, performance, and security of our Services, Communicate important account or service updates, Comply with legal, contractual, regulatory, accounting, and tax obligations, Protect the rights, property, and security of Nexus Tune, our users, and third parties; and Enforce our Terms & Conditions, Distribution Agreement, and other applicable policies.

12. Subscription Billing and Payment Processing

Where you purchase a Subscription or pay for any Service, Nexus Tune may process information necessary to complete the transaction, including billing details and payment records.

Payment transactions may be processed by independent third-party payment providers.

Nexus Tune does not store full payment card details where payment processing is performed by authorized payment service providers.

Payment providers process your information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

13. Royalty Administration

Where applicable, Nexus Tune processes Personal Data and rights-related information for the purposes of: Royalty calculations, Royalty reporting, Royalty payments, Revenue reconciliation, Tax compliance, Payment verification, Rights administration; and Financial record keeping.

Royalty information may be retained for accounting, legal, audit, and reporting purposes even after termination of your Account where required by law or legitimate business needs.

14. Publishing Administration

Where Publishing Administration services are provided, Nexus Tune may process information relating to: Songwriters, Composers, Lyricists, Publishers, Ownership splits, Collection societies, Publishing registrations, Mechanical rights, Performance rights; and Other publishing-related information necessary to provide the Services.

15. Rights Management Services

Where Rights Management services are provided, Nexus Tune may process Personal Data and Content-related information to:

Identify unauthorized uses: Administer digital rights, Submit Content to Content ID or similar systems, Process ownership information, Resolve rights disputes, Protect intellectual property; and Comply with platform or legal requirements.

16. Information Sharing

Nexus Tune does not sell your Personal Data.

We may share Personal Data only where reasonably necessary to operate our Services, comply with legal obligations, or protect legitimate business interests.

Recipients may include: Digital Service Providers (DSPs), Payment processors, Banking institutions, Rights management partners, Publishing administrators, Collection societies, Cloud hosting providers, Customer support providers, Analytics providers, Identity verification providers, Fraud prevention providers, Professional advisers, Auditors, Legal representatives; and Government authorities where required by applicable law.

Each recipient receives only the information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

17. Digital Service Providers (DSPs)

Where you request distribution of Content, Nexus Tune may provide relevant information to supported DSPs, including: Artist names, Release information, Metadata, Contributor information, Copyright notice, ISRCs, UPCs, Artwork, Audio files, Video files; and Other information reasonably required for digital distribution.

The processing of such information by DSPs is governed by their respective terms and privacy policies.

18. Service Providers

Nexus Tune may engage trusted third-party service providers to perform services on our behalf, including: Website hosting, Cloud infrastructure, Data storage, Customer support, Email delivery, Payment processing, Fraud prevention, Security monitoring, Analytics, Business communications; and Technical maintenance.

Such providers are required to process Personal Data only as necessary to provide the requested services and in accordance with applicable legal obligations.

19. Legal Disclosure

Nexus Tune may disclose Personal Data where reasonably necessary to: Comply with applicable law, Respond to lawful requests from public authorities, Enforce our agreements, Protect our legal rights, Investigate suspected fraud or unlawful conduct, Protect the safety of our users or the public; or Defend legal claims.

20. International Data Transfers

As Nexus Tune provides Services globally, Personal Data may be processed or transferred to countries outside your country of residence.

Such transfers may occur where reasonably necessary to: Provide the Services, Distribute Content internationally, Process royalty payments, Engage trusted service providers, Comply with legal obligations; or Support our worldwide business operations.

Where required by applicable law, Nexus Tune will implement appropriate safeguards designed to protect Personal Data during international transfers.

21. Data Security

Nexus Tune maintains reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect Personal Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure, or unauthorized access.

Security measures may include: Access controls, Authentication procedures, Encryption where appropriate, Secure communication protocols, Monitoring systems, Backup procedures; an, Security assessments.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

22. Data Retention

Nexus Tune retains Personal Data only for as long as reasonably necessary to: Provide the Services, Maintain business records, Process royalties, Resolve disputes, Comply with tax, accounting, and legal obligations, Detect fraud; or Protect our legitimate business interests.

Retention periods may vary depending upon the nature of the information and applicable legal requirements.

Where appropriate, Personal Data may be anonymized or securely deleted once it is no longer required.

23. Business Transfers

If Nexus Tune undergoes a merger, acquisition, corporate restructuring, sale of assets, financing transaction, or similar business event, Personal Data may be transferred to the relevant successor or acquiring entity, subject to applicable law.

24. Security Incidents
