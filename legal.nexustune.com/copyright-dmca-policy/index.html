Copyright & DMCA Policy

Nexus Tune Official Legal & Compliance Document • Published 2026

1. Introduction

Nexus Tune respects intellectual property rights and expects all users of our Services to do the same.

This Copyright & DMCA Policy explains how Nexus Tune responds to copyright infringement notices, ownership disputes, trademark complaints, and other intellectual property matters relating to the Services.

This Policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions, Distribution Agreement, Privacy Policy, Refund & Subscription Policy, Cookie Policy, AI Music Policy, and any other applicable Nexus Tune policies.

2. Copyright Ownership

Users remain solely responsible for ensuring that they own or have obtained all necessary rights, licenses, permissions, and authorizations required to distribute, publish, monetize, or otherwise use any Content submitted to Nexus Tune.

Submitting Content to Nexus Tune does not transfer ownership of your copyrights unless otherwise agreed in writing.

3. User Representations

By submitting Content, you represent and warrant that:

  • You own or control all necessary rights.
  • Your Content does not infringe any copyright, trademark, publicity, privacy, or other legal rights.
  • All required licenses and permissions have been obtained.
  • The submitted metadata is accurate and complete.
  • Distribution of the Content complies with applicable law and DSP policies.

4. Copyright Infringement Notices

If you believe that Content distributed through Nexus Tune infringes your copyright, you may submit a written copyright complaint containing:

  • Your full legal name;
  • Contact information;
  • Identification of the copyrighted work;
  • Identification of the allegedly infringing Content;
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is unauthorized;
  • A statement that the information provided is accurate; and
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Incomplete notices may delay processing.

5. Review Process

Upon receiving a valid complaint, Nexus Tune may:

  • Request additional information;
  • Contact the parties involved;
  • Temporarily suspend the affected Content;
  • Hold related royalty payments where appropriate;
  • Remove or disable access to Content where required by law or platform policy; or
  • Reject complaints that appear incomplete, unsupported, or abusive.

Submission of a complaint does not guarantee removal of Content.

6. Counter Notification

If your Content has been removed or disabled as a result of a copyright complaint and you believe the removal was made in error, you may submit a counter-notification containing sufficient information to support your claim.

Nexus Tune may forward a valid counter-notification to the complaining party where appropriate and may restore the Content if permitted by applicable law and platform policies.

7. Ownership Disputes

Where multiple parties claim ownership of the same Content, Nexus Tune may request documentation such as:

  • Copyright registrations;
  • License agreements;
  • Publishing agreements;
  • Split sheets;
  • Recording agreements;
  • Court orders; or
  • Other evidence reasonably necessary to verify ownership.

Until the dispute is resolved, Nexus Tune may suspend distribution or place related royalty payments on hold.

8. Trademark Complaints

Nexus Tune may also investigate complaints relating to trademarks, trade names, logos, artist names, or branding.

Users must ensure that submitted Content does not unlawfully use or infringe the intellectual property rights of others.

9. Repeat Infringer Policy

Nexus Tune reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts that repeatedly submit Content found to infringe intellectual property rights or repeatedly violate this Policy.

Repeated violations may result in:

  • Removal of Content;
  • Suspension of distribution services;
  • Account suspension;
  • Permanent termination of the Account; and
  • Restriction from using Nexus Tune Services in the future.

10. False or Fraudulent Claims

Knowingly submitting false, misleading, fraudulent, or abusive copyright complaints or counter-notifications may result in:

  • Rejection of the claim;
  • Suspension of account privileges;
  • Termination of Services; and
  • Other actions permitted under applicable law.

Users remain responsible for the accuracy of any information submitted.

11. Cooperation with Authorities

Nexus Tune may cooperate with courts, government authorities, law enforcement agencies, Digital Service Providers (DSPs), payment providers, and rights holders where required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

12. Limitation of Responsibility

Nexus Tune acts as a technology and distribution service provider.

Except as required by applicable law, Nexus Tune does not independently verify ownership of every work submitted and relies on the representations, warranties, and documentation provided by users.

Nothing in this Policy limits any legal obligations imposed on Nexus Tune by applicable law.

13. Policy Updates

Nexus Tune may modify this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in applicable laws, industry standards, or operational practices.

The latest version will be published on the Nexus Tune website together with the updated "Last Updated" date.
