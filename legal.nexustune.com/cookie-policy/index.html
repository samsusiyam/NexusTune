Cookie Policy

Nexus Tune Official Legal & Compliance Document • Published 2026

1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Nexus Tune ("Nexus Tune," "we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you access or use our website, dashboard, applications, and related digital services (collectively, the "Services").

This Cookie Policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions, Distribution Agreement, Privacy Policy, Refund & Subscription Policy, AI Music Policy, Copyright & DMCA Policy, and any other applicable Nexus Tune policies.

By continuing to use our Services, you acknowledge that cookies may be used in accordance with this Cookie Policy and applicable law.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, smartphone, tablet, or other device when you visit a website.

Cookies help websites remember user preferences, improve security, enhance functionality, analyze website performance, and provide a better user experience.

Some cookies are placed by Nexus Tune, while others may be placed by trusted third-party service providers.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Nexus Tune may use the following categories of cookies:

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are necessary for the operation of our Services.

These cookies enable functions such as:

  • User authentication
  • Account login
  • Security
  • Session management
  • Fraud prevention
  • Payment verification
  • Dashboard functionality

These cookies cannot generally be disabled because they are necessary for the Services to operate.

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies remember your preferences and improve your experience by storing settings such as:

  • Language preferences
  • Region
  • Dashboard settings
  • User preferences
  • Display preferences

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use our Services.

Information collected may include:

  • Pages visited
  • Session duration
  • Navigation paths
  • Device information
  • Browser information
  • Website performance
  • Error reports

Analytics information is generally collected in aggregated or pseudonymized form where appropriate.

Performance Cookies

Performance cookies allow Nexus Tune to:

  • Improve website speed
  • Monitor system performance
  • Detect technical issues
  • Optimize user experience
  • Improve service reliability

Cookies

Security cookies help:

  • Prevent unauthorized access
  • Detect fraudulent activity
  • Protect user accounts
  • Monitor suspicious login attempts
  • Maintain website integrity

Marketing Cookies

Where permitted by applicable law, Nexus Tune may use marketing cookies to:

  • Measure advertising performance
  • Improve promotional campaigns
  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Measure marketing effectiveness

Marketing cookies will only be used where legally permitted and, where required, after obtaining your consent.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Certain third-party service providers may place cookies on our website, including providers involved in:

  • Payment processing
  • Website analytics
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security monitoring
  • Customer support
  • Marketing services
  • Embedded media
  • Social media integrations

These third parties process information in accordance with their own privacy and cookie policies.

Nexus Tune does not control the cookies placed by independent third parties.

5. Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, Nexus Tune may use similar technologies including:

  • Web Beacons
  • Pixel Tags
  • Local Storage
  • Session Storage
  • SDK Technologies (where applicable)
  • Device Identifiers
  • Similar tracking technologies permitted under applicable law

These technologies serve similar purposes to cookies.

6. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Operate our Services
  • Maintain secure user sessions
  • Improve website performance
  • Remember user preferences
  • Detect fraud
  • Protect user accounts
  • Analyze website traffic
  • Improve customer experience
  • Support technical functionality
  • Comply with legal obligations

7. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to:

  • Accept cookies
  • Reject cookies
  • Delete cookies
  • Block specific cookies
  • Receive notifications before cookies are stored

You may manage your browser settings at any time.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the availability or functionality of some Services.

8. Cookie Consent

Where required by applicable law, Nexus Tune will request your consent before placing non-essential cookies on your device.

You may withdraw or modify your cookie preferences at any time through available cookie management tools or browser settings.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

9. Cookie Retention

Some cookies remain active only during your browsing session and are automatically deleted when you close your browser.

Other cookies may remain on your device for a longer period depending upon:

  • Cookie purpose
  • Browser settings
  • User preferences
  • Applicable legal requirements

10. International Users

As Nexus Tune provides Services globally, cookies may be processed in connection with users located in multiple jurisdictions.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be implemented for international data transfers relating to cookie data.

11. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for children who are below the minimum legal age required to use our Services under applicable law.

We do not knowingly use cookies to intentionally collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

12. Changes to this Cookie Policy

Nexus Tune may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect:

  • Changes in technology
  • Legal requirements
  • Business practices
  • Security improvements
  • Service enhancements

The updated version will be published on the Nexus Tune website together with the revised "Last Updated" date.

Continued use of the Services after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised Cookie Policy.
